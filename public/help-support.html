Frequently Asked Questions

Account & Login
Enter your registered 10-digit mobile number on the login screen. You will receive a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) via SMS. Enter the OTP to log in securely without needing a password.
Please verify that your phone has good mobile network coverage and that SMS delivery is not blocked by DND settings. You can click "Resend OTP" after 60 seconds. If you still face issues, contact support@sensifycare.com.
Go to the Profile tab in the application. You can view and edit your name, date of birth, gender, height, weight, and lifestyle parameters at any time.
Health Score & Parameters
Your Health Score is dynamically calculated out of 100 based on your logged lifestyle metrics, including daily sleep hours, physical exercise frequency, hydration levels, diet quality, stress levels, and active symptoms.
We recommend reviewing your parameters weekly or whenever your routine changes (e.g., changes in sleep schedule, workout routine, or diet) to keep your health insights accurate.
Medical Reports & AI Analysis
You can upload clear photos (JPG, PNG) or document files (PDF) up to 10MB in size. Ensure that the text, test names, and numeric values in the document are clearly readable.
Yes, all uploaded health records are encrypted in transit and at rest. They are accessible only by you and are never shared with unauthorized parties or advertisers.
Reminders & Notifications
Navigate to the Reminders section in the app. Tap "Add Reminder", select the type (Medication, Hydration, Exercise, or Custom), choose the time and frequency, and save.
Ensure that notification permissions are enabled for Sensify Care in your phone's Settings. Also verify that Battery Optimization or Battery Saver is not killing background notification delivery for the app.
Data Privacy & Account Control
You can delete your account directly through the app by going to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. Alternatively, you can email support@sensifycare.com requesting account deletion. All your personal data, medical reports, and reminders will be permanently purged.

Important Medical Emergency Disclaimer

Sensify Care provides health monitoring, reminder tracking, and educational insights. It is NOT an emergency medical response service and should not replace consultation with a qualified medical professional. In case of an acute medical emergency, please call your local emergency medical service immediately.
